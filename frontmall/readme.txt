Frontmall - a premium, conversion-focused WooCommerce theme for the Kenyan and
international market. Fast, accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA), SEO-ready and built to help
pass Google Merchant Center policy checks.

== Included tools ==
* WooCommerce > Merchant Compliance - audits the store against Google Merchant
  Center policy (emphasis on Misrepresentation).
* Appearance > Frontmall Setup - install/activate the recommended plugin stack.
* On activation the theme auto-creates the required policy/support pages with
  real, editable content.
